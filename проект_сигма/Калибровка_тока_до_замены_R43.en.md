# Preliminary current calibration — 27 October 2026

[Русский, original TXT](Калибровка_тока_до_замены_R43.txt) | English

Historical report from **before R43 was replaced**. Its proposed next step was subsequently performed; see [the later calibration](Калибровка_тока_2026-10-27.en.md).

Working project: `Design1_parker_v0.dspproj`. Copy: `Design1_parker_current_preliminary.dspproj`. DSP sample rate: 96 kHz. Loaded into RAM; EEPROM was not written.

`CURRENT_INPUT_0_1`: command input 0..1, 5.23 format. `CURRENT_0_3A`: linear interpolator, Min=0, Max=1, 5 points. `ZERO_OFF`: when input <=0, both DAC0/DAC1 digital commands are zero. For input >0: DAC0=table(x), DAC1=-table(x). `ZERO_CONSTANT` must remain 0.

Slow current drift during warm-up was detected. A static table does not eliminate it. At the end, the command was set to 0; the multimeter read 0.000 A.

## Feedback was not calibrated at this stage

At zero current, ADC1 returned -1; at high currents, +0.999998. R43=180 Ω. A table cannot restore information lost to saturation. `ADC1_RAW_PENDING_R43` then performed an identity transform over -1..1, and `FB_NOT_CALIBRATED` showed raw ADC1, **not** normalized current.

The next hardware step proposed in this historical report was to replace R43 with 10 kΩ with power disconnected. Then remeasure the ADC1 range, check for absence of saturation, and calibrate its interpolator: 0 A → 0, 3 A → 1. Check ripple; average-current readings require averaging.

DAC3 0..10 V voltage calibration was preserved with unchanged coefficients. Measurement records were under `output/meter/current_*.json` (now `measurements/2026-10-27/`). `tools/sigma/install_*.py` are one-off construction scripts; do not rerun them.
