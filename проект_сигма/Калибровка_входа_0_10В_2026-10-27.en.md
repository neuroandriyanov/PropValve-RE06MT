# 0–10 V input calibration, ADC0 — 27 October 2026

[Русский, original TXT](Калибровка_входа_0_10В_2026-10-27.txt) | English

Historical report translated without changing its values. This temporary X3–X4 calibration connection must not be retained in the final control path; see the [current setup instructions](../docs/SETUP.en.md).

The user fitted an X3–X4 jumper and replaced R42 with 10 kΩ. Calibration used the connected multimeter in DC V mode. The source was the previously calibrated X3 output; measurements were made directly at the connected X3/X4. Eleven points were checked, including intermediate points and a repeated zero.

Working project: `Design1_parker_v0.dspproj`. Complete reference copy: `Design1_parker_io_calibrated.dspproj`. The project was loaded into RAM; EEPROM was not written.

## Input path

`Input1, ADC0 -> VIN_LPF_20Hz -> VIN_TABLE_0_10V -> VIN_LIMIT_MIN -> VIN_LIMIT_MAX -> VIN_MEAS_0_1`.

LPF: first order, 20 Hz, gain 0 dB. At Fs=96 kHz, the time constant is approximately 8 ms; settling to 1% after a step takes approximately 37 ms. `VIN_MEAS_0_1` is the normalized input: voltage in volts = reading * 10. `ADC0_RAW` is unsmoothed ADC0; `VIN_FILTERED` is smoothed ADC0 before normalization. The coil had a zero command during calibration. No connection from `VIN_MEAS_0_1` to the current command was added.

Table Min=-1, Max=+1. This is the ADC0 data range, not the voltage at X4. Over the working 0..10 V range, ADC0 varied approximately -0.02050..+0.74948. No saturation was found. The full table range was retained for margin; its output is separately limited to 0..1 by two conditional blocks. `VIN_ZERO=0`, `VIN_ONE=1`, `VIN_LIMIT_MIN` condition: `>`, `VIN_LIMIT_MAX`: `<`. Negative and greater-than-1 coefficients at the outer table knots are extrapolation and are valid. The final signal is limited to 0..1.
