0–10 V input calibration, ADC0 — 27.10.2026

The user installed an X3–X4 jumper and replaced R42 with 10 kΩ.
Calibration used the connected multimeter in DC V mode.
Source: the previously calibrated X3 output; measurements were taken directly
at connected X3/X4. 11 points were checked, including intermediate points and a return to zero.

Working project: Design1_parker_v0.dspproj.
Full reference copy: Design1_parker_io_calibrated.dspproj.
The project was downloaded to RAM; EEPROM was not written.

Input path:
Input1, ADC0 -> VIN_LPF_20Hz -> VIN_TABLE_0_10V -> VIN_LIMIT_MIN -> VIN_LIMIT_MAX -> VIN_MEAS_0_1.
Low-pass filter: first order, 20 Hz, gain 0 dB. At Fs=96 kHz the time constant
is about 8 ms; settling to within 1% after a step takes about 37 ms.
VIN_MEAS_0_1 is the normalized input: voltage in volts = reading * 10.
ADC0_RAW is unfiltered ADC0; VIN_FILTERED is filtered ADC0 before normalization.
The coil command was zero throughout calibration.
VIN_MEAS_0_1 was not connected to the current command.

Table Min = -1, Max = +1. This is the ADC0 data range, not X4 voltage.
Across the 0..10 V operating range, ADC0 varied approximately -0.02050..+0.74948.
No saturation was found. The full table range was retained for headroom;
its output is independently clamped to 0..1 by two conditional blocks.
VIN_ZERO=0, VIN_ONE=1; VIN_LIMIT_MIN condition: >; VIN_LIMIT_MAX condition: <.
Negative and above-1 coefficients at the table's outer nodes are valid
extrapolations. The final signal is clamped to 0..1.
