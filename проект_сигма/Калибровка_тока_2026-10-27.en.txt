CURRENT_INPUT_0_1 is the 0..1 command input, format 5.23.
CURRENT_0_3A is the current-command table, input range 0..1.
ZERO_OFF: when the command is <=0, both DAC0/DAC1 digital commands are zero.
For a positive command, DAC0=table(x), DAC1=-table(x).
ZERO_CONSTANT must remain zero.

Measurement:
ADC1 -> CURRENT_LPF_20Hz -> FB_LIMIT_MIN -> FB_LIMIT_MAX -> I_FEEDBACK_0_1 -> I_MEAS_0_1.
CURRENT_LPF_20Hz is a first-order low-pass filter, 20 Hz, gain 0 dB.
I_FEEDBACK_0_1 is a 5-point interpolator: Min=-0.78, Max=0.70.
FB_LIMIT_MIN/MAX explicitly clamp the interpolator input to -0.78..0.70.
Constants FB_MIN_CONST=-0.78, FB_MAX_CONST=0.70 must match Min/Max.
I_MEAS_0_1 is normalized measured current; amperes = value * 3.
ADC1_RAW is the diagnostic unfiltered ADC1 signal.
No digital regulation loop using I_MEAS_0_1 was closed;
the TL494 analog current controller continues to control the power stage.

After R43 replacement, no ADC1 saturation was found over 0..3 A:
at ~3 A the unfiltered signal was approximately +0.658 to +0.754.
Filtering reduces reading scatter but does not eliminate zero drift.
In one calibration series the zero level changed by about
0.013 ADC units, equivalent to ~26 mA.
A fixed table does not compensate zero changes between series.
This verification describes the board during those measurements;
it does not guarantee thermal accuracy after cooling or warming.
The interpolator itself does not clamp correctly outside Min/Max:
before FB_LIMIT_MIN/MAX were added, this caused erroneous readings at the ends.
Separate blocks before the interpolator now perform clamping.
CURRENT_INPUT_0_1 must be supplied within the stated 0..1 range.
