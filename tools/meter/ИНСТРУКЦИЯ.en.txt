Direct UNI-T UT61D+ reading without image capture
================================================

Russian original: ИНСТРУКЦИЯ.txt

Tested with a real WCH CH9329 adapter, VID:PID 1A86:E429.
The manufacturer's UT61D+ application remained open during the test.
Dependencies: Windows and Python 3; no additional packages or drivers are needed.
CP2110 support follows its specification but was not tested on this computer.

Commands from the project root:

  .\tools\meter\meter.ps1 --list
  .\tools\meter\meter.ps1 --count 1
  .\tools\meter\meter.ps1 --count 5 --interval 1
  .\tools\meter\meter.ps1 --count 0 --interval 1 --latest output/meter/latest.json --log output/meter/readings.jsonl

The last command runs until Ctrl+C. No background services, startup entries
or scheduled tasks are installed. The default is one measurement.
read_meter.py can be invoked directly with any installed Python 3.

Output is JSON: display, mode, unit, volts, amps, quantity, flags, timestamp_utc, received_unix,
checksum_valid, calibration_usable, frame_hex. Each measurement is requested
from the instrument. Packet length, header and checksum are checked.
The timestamp records receipt by the computer, not ADC conversion time.
The UT61D+ model was identified by the user and options.xml; the utility does
not query the model name automatically, to avoid interrupting existing polling.

With HOLD/REL/MIN/MAX/PEAK, low battery, overload or an unsuitable mode,
calibration_usable=false. For DC voltage and DC current,
calibration_usable can be true: the consumer must check quantity.
volts is populated only for voltage modes; amps only for current modes,
converting A/mA/uA to amperes. Units in other modes are not guessed.
Coil calibration requires quantity=current and DCA/DCmA/DCuA mode;
voltage calibration requires quantity=voltage and DCV/DCmV mode.

Reading latest.json alone does NOT prove that the instrument is connected now:
the last measurement remains there after the utility stops. Before calibration,
request a new measurement and check ok=true, calibration_usable=true,
checksum_valid=true and the age of received_unix (recommended maximum: 3 seconds).
If the next attempt fails, latest.json is replaced with an error message.

If the two applications' polling interferes, stop polling in the manufacturer
utility. The reading utility does not terminate other processes or change the
instrument's mode, range, HOLD, REL, EEPROM settings or USB identifiers.
Its only multimeter command is measurement request 0x5E.

Findings about the manufacturer application
------------------------------------------
DMM.exe is a 32-bit Qt 5 application. Settings are stored in options.xml.
CustomDmm.dll implements the multimeter protocol layer and command queues.
CP2110.dll and SLABHIDtoUART.dll provide the alternative Silicon Labs transport.
CH9329DLL.dll contains CH9329ReadData/CH9329WriteData and uses Windows HID.
The adapter actually found on this PC was CH9329, not CP2110. CP2110 DLLs
in the application folder do not establish that a CP2110 adapter is connected.

CH9329 uses a 65-byte Windows HID report:
  [00] [UART length] [UART data] [padding to 65 bytes]
UART measurement request:
  AB CD 03 5E 01 D9
UART measurement response:
  AB CD 10 [14 data bytes] [checksum, most-significant byte first]
The checksum is the sum of all UART-frame bytes except the last two, modulo 65536.
HID padding beyond the declared length may contain arbitrary bytes;
it must be ignored.

Captured real response (6.25 V DC):
  AB CD 10 02 31 20 20 20 36 2E 32 35 00 03 30 30 30 03 79

PE-file import/string analysis and on-device protocol verification were sufficient.
IDA, COM interception and modification of manufacturer files were unnecessary.
The inspect_vendor.py inspector does not execute the DLLs being examined.
