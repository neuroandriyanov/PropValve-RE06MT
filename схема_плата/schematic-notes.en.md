# Schematic and BOM language versions

[Русский](schematic-notes.md) | **English**

The original files are preserved: [схема.pdf](схема.pdf) and [Спецификация_для_пайк.xlsx](Спецификация_для_пайк.xlsx). English copies: [schematic.en.pdf](schematic.en.pdf) and [Спецификация_для_пайк.en.xlsx](Спецификация_для_пайк.en.xlsx).

Only two labels on the first schematic sheet were translated: “ошибка” above DA7.2 → `error`, and `330 2Вт` beside R5 → `330 2W`. The original vector drawing, connections, reference designators and component values are preserved. The second sheet is unchanged.

The English BOM translates headings, sections, notes, price bases and unit labels. Numbers, formulas, source links and historical prices are preserved. The three worksheet names remain stable for formula references: “Порядок пайки” — Soldering order, “Исходные данные” — Source data, “Цены и источники” — Prices and sources. The price-check date in the workbook belongs to the original document; translation does not imply a new check of supplier offers.

Translation does not resolve technical discrepancies between sources. The current PDF schematic specifies R42 and R43 as `10k`; the historical BOM and early photographs include `180`. The annotation beside DA7.2 and the visible wire rework retain their original meaning. For the current project state, see the [README](README.en.md) and [status](../docs/STATUS.en.md).
